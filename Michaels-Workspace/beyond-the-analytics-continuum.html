The Research Question

Why the traditional analytics maturity model may not be enough

The Traditional Continuum

Analytics capability is commonly organized as a linear progression — descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive. Harrix Research is investigating whether this model adequately represents organizational analytics capability.

The Core Thesis

Organizations may possess advanced analytical tools while lacking the organizational capabilities required to create value from them. Analytics maturity may need to be evaluated across multiple organizational dimensions — not analytics type alone.

The Proposed Framework

The emerging Enterprise Intelligence Capability Matrix examines analytics capability across multiple organizational dimensions — not as a single linear scale, but as a structured assessment model.

Research Methodology

A structured Rapid Evidence Assessment

The research program uses a structured Rapid Evidence Assessment to examine academic and practitioner literature related to analytics maturity, organizational capabilities, decision-making, and competitive advantage.
